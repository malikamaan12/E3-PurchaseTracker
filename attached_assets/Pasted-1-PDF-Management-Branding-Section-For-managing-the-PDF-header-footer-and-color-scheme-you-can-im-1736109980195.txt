1. PDF Management Branding Section
For managing the PDF header, footer, and color scheme, you can implement customization options in your system. Here’s a structure you can include:

PDF Header and Footer Management:
Header Customization:
Add text, logos, or images to the header.
Options to define font size, color, and alignment (left, center, right).
Option to insert date, page number, or document title automatically.
Footer Customization:
Add custom footer text or branding.
Font size, color, and alignment.
Option to add page numbering, document metadata, or copyright information.
Color Scheme:
Primary Color:
Set the primary color for the header and footer sections of the PDF (e.g., brand colors for consistency).
Secondary Color:
Add an accent color for borders, separators, or highlights within the header and footer.
Font Style:
Choose from a list of pre-defined fonts (e.g., Arial, Helvetica, Times New Roman) for the header and footer text.
Text Alignment and Spacing:
Set padding and margin for text in the header/footer to ensure legibility and brand consistency.
2. Overall Color of the Dashboard
For the overall color scheme of the dashboard, choose colors that align with your brand identity while ensuring a clean, user-friendly interface. You can follow this structure:

Background Color:

Light or dark theme options (e.g., light gray or white for a clean, minimalist look; or dark blue/black for a more professional, modern appearance).
Primary Action Buttons:

Use a bright accent color (e.g., blue, orange, or green) for buttons that encourage user interaction, like "Save," "Publish," or "Download."
Text Color:

For readability, use dark colors (e.g., black or dark gray) for most text, and lighter shades for secondary text or instructions.
Borders and Dividers:

Subtle borders (e.g., light gray) for separating sections or input fields.
Notification and Alert Colors:

Use red or orange for error messages and green for success messages to guide users clearly.
3. Logo and Notice on the Dashboard
Logo Placement:

Place your logo prominently at the top left or center of the dashboard header. This could be a clickable logo that redirects to the home page of your application.
Notice Section:

Add a notice or alert section below the logo or at the top of the sidebar. This can be used to display important messages or updates, like system alerts, maintenance notices, or announcements.

Notice Design:

Use a subtle background color (e.g., light yellow or blue) for the notice section, with bold text for emphasis.
The notice can be dismissible or remain until the user acknowledges it.
